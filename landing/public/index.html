How a show happens

  1. Propose

    Name who you want to see and the city. Fans, performers, organisers and sponsors can all propose a show. If someone already asked for it, your vote joins theirs.

  2. Back it

    Pledge what a ticket is worth to you. It's a promise, not a payment, so it costs nothing to show support.

  3. Hit the target

    Each show has a target. Share the page with friends, because every backer moves the bar.

  4. Book the show

    We bring the numbers to the performer's team, line up the venue and production, and you get first access to tickets.

Built for everyone who makes a show

Performers

Artists, bands, orchestras and theatre companies can see where their audience really is, propose their own shows, and play to rooms that already want them.

Organisers

Promoters can test an idea before booking anything, from a one-night concert to a touring production.

Sponsors

Back the shows your audience is asking for, with proof of demand before you commit a budget.

Venues and crews

Venues, sound, staging, security and ticketing can offer their services for shows that already have a crowd waiting.
