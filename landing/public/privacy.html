Your privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026. This notice covers the HiveConcerts pre-launch page only.

HiveConcerts is getting ready to launch. Before then, this page lets you tell us who you want to see live and in which city. This notice explains what we collect when you do that and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Who you want to see, the kind of show, and the city you name.
  • Whether you joined as a fan, a performer or their team, an organiser, a sponsor, or a venue or event service.
  • Your email address.
  • The country your connection comes from, worked out by our hosting provider. We don't store your full location.
  • A scrambled (hashed) version of your IP address, used only to block automated spam. It can't be turned back into your IP address.

We don't use advertising trackers on this page, and we don't set cookies for tracking.

Why we collect it

  • To count how many people want each show in each city, so we can decide where HiveConcerts launches and which shows to pursue first.
  • To email you about the show you asked for and about the HiveConcerts launch. We don't send anything else.
  • To keep the vote counts honest by limiting spam.

We rely on your consent when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who can see it

Only the HiveConcerts team. We never sell your information or share your email with performers, sponsors or anyone else. Performers and their teams may see totals, such as how many people want them in a city, but not who those people are.

Our hosting and database run on Cloudflare, which processes the data on our behalf and may store it in data centres outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep your vote and email until HiveConcerts launches. If you don't create an account within 12 months of launch, we delete your email and keep only the anonymous count.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Every email we send will include an unsubscribe link.

Email privacy@hiveconcerts.com from the address you signed up with and tell us what you'd like. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

When the full HiveConcerts app launches, it will have its own privacy policy. If we change this notice before then, we'll update the date at the top.
